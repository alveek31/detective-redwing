        How to play

        Every day there is one new murder at Redwing Manor. Five guests, five items, five rooms. One guest is the killer.

        1. Read the scene and the evidence. Use the dossier: height, handedness, how heavy an item is, and whether a room is indoors.
        2. Crack the coded evidence. Anagrams hide a surname, trivia points to one item or room, and coded notes have every letter moved 3 along the alphabet (so D means A).
        3. Mark your notebook. Each guest pairs with exactly one item and one room.
        4. Accuse a suspect, weapon and location. You have 6 accusations.
        5. A wrong accusation tells you nothing except that you were wrong, and unseals a hint.

        The opening evidence is always enough: pure logic reaches exactly one answer before any hint. A sharp detective solves it in one.
