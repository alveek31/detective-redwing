      How to play

      You are Detective Redwing. Every day brings one new case. Some days it is a murder, some days a theft, and the scene moves around: Hawthorne Manor, the ocean liner SS Aurelia, the Hotel Edelweiss in the Alps and the Silver Arrow night train. Five guests, five items, five rooms. One guest is the culprit.

      1. Read the scene and the evidence. Use the dossier: height, handedness, how heavy an item is, and whether a room is indoors.
      2. Crack the coded evidence. Each day of the week brings different codes: anagrams, trivia, general knowledge (Merlot and Malbec point to the Wine Cellar), number codes, Morse, mirror writing and shifted-letter notes. Coded notes come with a decoder: you work out which tool cracks it. Anagrams come with letter tiles, and number and Morse codes with a code card.
      3. Mark your notebook. Each guest pairs with exactly one item and one room. On a phone, the evidence card above the notebook lets you read and mark at the same time, and tapping a clue's number sets it aside once you've used it.
      4. In a murder, name the killer, the weapon and the room. In a theft, name the thief, the piece they stole and the room they took it from. You have 6 accusations.
      5. A wrong accusation tells you nothing except that you were wrong, and unseals a hint. If all six miss, the answer is unsealed the next day, once the case has closed in every time zone.

      The opening evidence is always enough: pure logic reaches exactly one answer before any hint. A sharp detective solves it in one.
