    How to play
    • The guests called ahead to warn the host about each other. Seat everyone so the dinner goes smoothly.
    • Drag a guest onto a chair, or tap a guest and then tap a chair. Drop a guest anywhere away from the chairs, or tap a seated guest, to send them back to the bar.
    • "Next to" means the chairs on either side. "Across" means directly opposite.
    • The chair at the top of the table is the head. The window is on the right wall and the door is on the left.
    • Speed is your score. You have three wine glasses. Each wrong "Serve dinner" breaks one, and the third ruins dinner.
    • A new dinner every day. Monday is gentle, the weekend is a full house.
